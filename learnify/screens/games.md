## Game modes

    (lightning)  Blitz 60
    Answer as many civics questions as you can in 60 seconds
    — quick correct answers build a combo for bonus points

    (heart)  Interview Survival
    Play by the real interview rule: 9 wrong answers and you
    are out. Reach 12 correct for a PASS — then keep going
    for a record

    (calendar)  Daily Sprint
    10 fresh questions every day — keep your streak alive and
    warm up before real practice

    (cards)  Memory Match
    Flip two cards at a time and pair each question with its
    answer — fewer moves, better score

    (bubbles)  Balloon Pop
    Two balloons rise: TRUE and FALSE. Read the statement and
    pop the correct balloon before they float away

    (A-check)  Word Forge
    Train for the real writing test — tap the letter tiles to
    spell each official vocabulary word

    (magnifier)  Who Am I?
    Guess the historical figure from a clue — answer before
    the second clue for double points
